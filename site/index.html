iOS 26+Apple IntelligenceOn-device by default

ChatGPT-style AI that works in airplane mode

Chat and ask questions about your own documents using Apple's on-device model. No Applebot server. No account. Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you connect a service or switch on an external model yourself.

On-device by default

Runs on the Apple Intelligence model built into your iPhone. Works with no signal until you opt into the network.

Chat with your documents

Import PDFs, notes, and photos. Answers cite their sources, and you can see exactly what the model read.

Connectors, with approval

Ask about your email, schedule, weather, stock prices or the web. Drafts and events are shown to you first and only happen if you approve. Applebot never sends email.

Optional OpenAI or Anthropic

In Settings, switch chats to your own API key when you want a larger cloud model. Keys stay in the Keychain; turn it off anytime.

Private by design

No telemetry, encrypted at rest, optional Face ID lock, and one tap to delete everything.

Built for a small model

Smart context budgeting, rolling summaries, and hybrid search make the most of a compact on-device model.

Requirements

iOS 26 or later on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer), with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.
