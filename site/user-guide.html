Applebot Features User guide Changelog

User guide

Applebot is a chat assistant that runs on your iPhone by default. With the optional external model off, it works in airplane mode and never sends your messages or documents anywhere. The only exceptions are a connector or an external model you switch on, each of which talks directly from your iPhone to that service.

Requirements

  • iOS 26 or later
  • An iPhone with Apple Intelligence: iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 or newer
  • Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. The first time, iOS downloads the model; Applebot shows a banner until it is ready.

Theme and text contrast

Open You → Theme and choose an appearance you find comfortable: System (follows iOS Light/Dark), Daylight, Midnight, Ocean, Forest, or High contrast. Each option sets accent colour and readable text on its canvas.

Chatting

  1. Open the Chat tab and tap the compose button (or Start chatting). Each new chat is a brand new conversation with no memory of other chats.
  2. Type a message and tap the arrow. The answer streams in as it is generated. Tap the stop button to interrupt.
  3. Long-press an answer to copy or share it.
  4. Swipe a chat right to pin it, or left to delete it. Long-press a row for the same actions. Use the search field to find chats by title or content.
  5. Inside an open chat, open the ⋯ menu and choose Delete Chat, then confirm. The conversation is removed from this iPhone and you return to the chat list.

Start a new conversation

Tap the compose button in the chat's top bar to start a fresh conversation at any time. Your current chat is saved in the list, and the new one starts empty: no history, no summary, and document search reset to all documents. The button is greyed out while the current chat is still empty, and a chat that never receives a message is not saved.

Delete a conversation

  • From the list: swipe left (or long-press → Delete).
  • From inside a chat: ⋯ → Delete Chat → confirm.

Deletion removes that chat’s messages and summary from on-device storage. It does not erase long-term memory facts, documents, or other chats.

Long conversations are handled automatically: older messages are condensed into a short summary so the model can keep going within its small context window.

Memory across chats

In You → Personal → What Applebot remembers, leave Remember across chats on so Applebot can keep short facts on this iPhone and use them in every chat. You can pin or delete each fact. Events and open loops expire unless pinned. Preferences wait for confirmation under Your profile.

Personal profile, brief, voice notes, rewrite

  • Your profile: name, city, roles, reply style, boundaries, and preference confirmations.
  • Behavioral patterns: optional on-device card of typical sleep, exercise, calendar work window, chat interests, travel affinity, stress tone, and spending hints. Refresh from Health & Calendar under You → Personal.
  • Morning brief on unlock (optional): once a day from Calendar and open loops.
  • Voice note… in a chat’s + menu: speak, review, save into the Voice notes document pack.
  • Write like me: add samples under You, then send rewrite this: … in a chat.

Health

Open the Health tab to connect Apple Health, see today’s activity and a 7-day wellness trend, review an awareness note, ask Applebot from Health into Chat, and manage medications (manual or label photo, optional local reminders, Taken on the row or notification). Use Siri or Shortcuts: “Mark metformin taken in Applebot” or “What’s my wellness score in Applebot”. Morning brief can include health lines. Medications stay in Applebot only. Applebot is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Stocks

Open the Stocks tab to enable Yahoo quotes, manage a private watchlist and optional holdings (shares and average cost — not broker-synced), review awareness and Ask Applebot prompts, and create price or event alerts (confirm suggested ones before they save). Morning brief can include markets lines. Siri or Shortcuts: open watchlist, ask about a ticker, or ask about portfolio. Quotes can be delayed. Applebot is not a broker and does not give financial advice.

Packs, family, check-ins, backup, automations

  • Library → Packs: filter, create packs, move documents.
  • Family profile: multiple people on one iPhone; Face ID to switch.
  • Gentle check-ins: optional soft prompts about open loops.
  • Helpful? thumbs on document answers retune on-device search.
  • Backup & restore: encrypted .applebotpack with your password.
  • Automations: e.g. every Sunday week plan — offered on unlock, runs only if you confirm.

Voice

Tap the mic in a chat to speak. Recognition stays on-device; if that is unavailable, the mic stays off. Enable Speak replies and optionally Hands-free conversation under You → Voice. Hands-free only reopens the mic after a voice turn — typing never starts it.

Conversation style and emotional chat

In You → Conversation style, choose how Applebot talks:

  • Warm companion (default): kind and attentive. It answers “how are you?” in character, listens first, and acknowledges how you feel.
  • Neutral: concise and factual.
  • Supportive coach: encouraging and practical, with one small next step.

In the warm and coach styles, Applebot reads the mood of each message on your iPhone and adapts. If you are clearly upset it skips the document search so it can simply talk with you. Empty chats ask "How are you feeling today?".

Applebot is not a therapist. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.

External model (OpenAI or Anthropic)

Applebot uses Apple Intelligence on this iPhone unless you switch on an external model.

  1. Open You → External model.
  2. Choose OpenAI or Anthropic, paste your API key, and optionally pick a model.
  3. Turn on Use external model.

Chats then call that provider over the network. A small banner in the chat reminds you that messages leave this iPhone. Turn the switch off any time to go back to the on-device model. Keys stay in the Keychain. You can also set a custom base URL for an OpenAI-compatible proxy or local server.

Connectors: accounts and live lookups

Connectors let Applebot look things up in your accounts and on the web when you ask, and propose actions that you approve first. They are off until you switch them on.

  1. Open You → Connected services → Connectors.
  2. Connect Calendar, Reminders, Apple Health, Location, or Gmail, or turn on Use in chats for Weather, Stocks, or Web search.
  3. For wilderness / no-signal use, open the Trail tab: connect Location, tap Download to choose an area on the map (like Google Maps), download while you have signal, and save waypoints. Optionally connect Motion for a sedentary check, and open AR trail overlay (LiDAR mesh is Pro-only; Air shows a warning and disables mesh). See docs/wilderness.md.
  4. For accessibility scene description, open the Surroundings tab, accept the safety note, allow Camera, then tap Describe what’s around me or turn on Live mode for continuous spoken updates (see docs/surroundings.md).
  5. For a 3D room model on a LiDAR Pro iPhone, open the Scan tab, walk the room with RoomPlan, then save/share a USDZ (see docs/scan.md). Disabled on Air.
  6. Ask in a chat. For example:
    You askWhat happens
    "What's on my calendar tomorrow?"Your calendar is read and summarised
    "How many steps did I take today?"Apple Health is read on this iPhone and summarised
    "What allergies are in my health records?"Clinical records from Apple Health (when linked)
    "Any emails from Alice this week?"Gmail is searched and the results are summarised
    "What's the weather in Paris?"Open-Meteo returns current conditions and a short forecast
    "What's AAPL at?"Yahoo Finance returns the latest price and change
    "What did the Fed decide this week?"Brave Search returns titles and snippets to summarise
    "Reply to Alice's lunch email saying yes"Applebot writes a reply and shows you the draft to approve
    "Add lunch with Sam tomorrow at 12:30"An approval sheet shows the event; it is added only if you approve

Approvals

Reading and live lookups happen straight away. Anything that changes something, such as saving a Gmail draft or adding a calendar event, shows a sheet with the exact content (recipient, subject and text, or the event's title, time and place). It only happens if you tap the green button. Don't allow, dismissing the sheet, or leaving the chat all mean no.

Applebot never sends email. It can only save a draft in Gmail for you to review and send yourself. Weather, Stocks, Web search and Apple Health are read-only. Location GPS stays on-device; saving a waypoint needs approval. Health answers are informational only — not medical advice. Trail navigation is not a certified wilderness instrument.

Connectors and documents

Connectors work in chats that are not searching your documents. If you have documents in your library, a notice in the chat offers to use your active connectors instead.

Setup

Calendar, Reminders, Apple Health, Location, Weather and Stocks need no build-time keys (Health uses the HealthKit entitlement already in the project). Gmail needs a Google OAuth client of type iOS with bundle ID dev.kekaflow.applebot, then GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=<your id> make project. Web search needs a Brave Search API key and BRAVE_API_KEY=<key> make project. Full steps are in docs/connectors.md and docs/wilderness.md.

Chat with your documents

Add documents

  1. Open the Documents tab and tap +.
  2. Choose Import files (PDF, text, Markdown, RTF, CSV, JSON, YAML, or images such as photos of paper documents) or Paste text.
  3. Applebot reads the text, splits it into passages, and indexes them on the device. Scanned PDFs and images use on-device text recognition (OCR).

Re-importing the same content is detected and skipped. Originals are not copied into the app; only extracted text and search vectors are stored.

Attach documents to a chat

Tap the + button next to the message box:

OptionWhat it does
Files…Pick PDFs, text, Markdown, and other supported files
Photos…Pick photos or screenshots of documents; text is read with on-device OCR
Paste text…Add a note by pasting text
From my library…Choose documents you already imported

Imported documents are added to your library and attached to this chat. They appear as chips above the message box, and this chat then searches only the attached documents. Tap a chip to change the selection, or tap its x to remove it. Removing every attached document goes back to searching all documents. Other chats are not affected.

Ask questions

  • With documents in your library, new chats use them automatically. The chat's toolbar menu has Use my documents to turn this on or off.
  • Choose Search all documents or pick specific documents to restrict the search.
  • Answers cite passages like [1]. Tap a source chip, or Why this answer?, to see the search query and every passage given to the model.
  • If the documents do not contain the answer, Applebot says so instead of guessing. When an answer should cite your passages but does not, Applebot quietly retries once. A small on-device model can still make mistakes, so check important facts against the source.

Specific questions work best ("What is the late fee on invoices?"). For broad requests, choose one document in the search scope and ask for a summary.

Privacy and security

  • On-device by default. No Applebot servers, accounts, or analytics. With connectors and the external model off there are no network requests at all. A connector or external model you switch on talks directly from your iPhone to that service and nowhere else.
  • Connector safety. Sign-in uses Google's secure sheet (Applebot never sees your password) and the token is kept in the iOS Keychain. Email text is treated as untrusted, and nothing is changed without your approval.
  • Encrypted at rest. Chats and the document library live in a file-protected folder.
  • App lock. In You → Privacy, turn on Require Face ID / passcode to lock the app whenever it goes to the background.
  • Delete everything. You → Delete all chats and documents erases all data Applebot stores and signs out of connected accounts.

Limits to be aware of

  • The on-device model is much smaller than cloud chatbots. It is best at summarizing, rewriting, extracting facts from your documents, brainstorming, and simple Q&A. It is weak at math, code, and recent world knowledge.
  • Its context window is about 4,000 tokens, shared by the conversation, retrieved passages, and the answer.
  • The model may decline some requests because of Apple's safety guardrails. Applebot then asks you to put it another way.
